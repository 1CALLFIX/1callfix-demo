## What's in it
Owner 2026-11-22: "while posting an ad there is no option as Post Now and also we can add post time also like today
morning 10 am".

- Post editor (Admin → Marketing → Social posts → ＋ New post / Edit):
  - **🚀 Post now** next to 📅 Schedule (HQ only; a franchise post still waits for HQ approval). Asks once ("Post
    this everywhere now?"), saves the post for this minute and sends it at once to every place that posts by itself
    (Direct or Buffer). Places set to By hand (WhatsApp Status) show under Today with their Share button. The message
    says where it went, what needs a tap, and any place that refused (with the reason; Try again on the card).
  - **5 · When**: Today / Tomorrow chips show which is picked; time chips **🌅 Morning 10 AM · ☀️ Noon 12 PM ·
    🌇 Evening 6 PM · 🌙 Night 8 PM**, or any time in the box.
  - Scheduling for a time that already passed today says "goes out in the next few minutes" (it always did go out
    at the next run; now it says so).
