## What's in it
### Careers page (owner 2026-11-21)
- **/careers**: hero, "Why work with us", the open jobs as cards (team colour, type, place, people needed, pay only
  when filled in), a band sending riders / technicians / shops to /partners (partners are not staff), the 4 hiring
  steps, and an "any role" application form. Share button. In the footer (Company → Careers) and the sitemap.
- **/careers/{job}**: the job's details (what you'll do / what we're looking for / what you get), Share, Google
  JobPosting data, and the application form (an "Apply now" bar stays at the bottom on phones until the form shows).
  A closed, expired or removed job keeps its page with "This job is closed" and the open jobs. A changed address
  301s from the old one. /jobs and /jobs/{job} 301 to /careers…
- **Application form**: job, name, mobile, city (Nellore filled in), experience chips, two-wheeler Yes/No (only for jobs
  that need one), optional CV (PDF ≤ 5 MB, private disk `storage/app/private/careers/cv`), optional note, consent.
  Honeypot, 5 per mobile per hour, phone check. One application per mobile and job; applying again updates it and
  keeps its status.
- **3 launch jobs** (seeded once, pay blank): Partner Onboarding Executive (Field) ×3, Customer Support Executive
  (Telugu & English) ×2, City Operations & Marketing Manager ×1 (digital marketing included, owner decision).
- **Admin → Website & Pages → Careers page**: add / edit / close / reopen / reorder / remove jobs, editable web address
  (old one keeps working), page on/off. **Admin → Users & Partners → Job applications**: filters, Call / WhatsApp,
  status (New, Shortlisted, Interview, Hired, Rejected), team notes, CV (opens only for the team, logged), CSV export.
  Permission `careers.manage` (Super Admin + roles that have `partner_page.manage`). Every change audit-logged.
- Page words: Admin → Website text & language → "Careers page" (English + Telugu).
- Test-login rule: not applicable (no new app or role).
