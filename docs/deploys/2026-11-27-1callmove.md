## What's in it (plain words)
Owner 2026-11-27: the transport side is **Transport · by 1CallFix** ("Send anything. Move anything."). **One brand, no
1Call… sub-brands** (owner: otherwise every module would need 1CallShop, 1CallFood …; one name is easier to trust,
search and protect from fake apps). Built on
the parcel module (no new module, no new app). **Nothing changes for customers until a 3-wheeler or truck fare is
set** (Admin → Parcel Settings → "Vehicle: …"), and today none is, so after this deploy the site looks exactly as before.

Once a 3-wheeler or truck can be booked in the customer's area:
- **Home "Explore" row**: the Parcel door says **Transport** with a new picture (a parcel on a mini truck), in the
  sticky bar too. Short on purpose: longer names ("Parcel + Movers") do not fit under the icon on phones. A picture the
  Super Admin uploaded for the Parcel door still wins. Only parcels live = "Parcel", as before.
- **Booking page `/parcel`**: a blue **Transport · by 1CallFix** header (brand colours of the logo pill) and
  "What do you want to do?" with big colourful buttons, **only for what can be booked here**:
  📦 Send a parcel · 🛺 Book a 3-wheeler · 🚚 Book a truck · 🏠 Shift house.
  - *Book a truck* starts on the smallest truck priced; "Truck size" cards switch between mini truck / pickup / truck.
  - *Shift house* picks a truck, "House shifting" and **2 helpers** (where the helper price is set; customer can change).
  - Each button shows the trip price once pickup and drop are chosen.
  - A link can open a button: `/parcel?want=shift` (or `parcel`, `three_wheeler`, `truck`), e.g. for a banner.
    A button not offered in that area is ignored (opens on parcels).
- **Words** are the Super Admin's, English + Telugu: Admin → Growth → Website text & language, new group
  "Transport page" (heading, tagline, button names) and Homepage → "Parcel door once a 3-wheeler or
  truck can be booked".
- Restaurant "book a rider for your own order" (`?from=store`) is unchanged (no buttons).
- **Rules kept**: parcel packs (a benefit) still cover two-wheeler parcels only; no coupons on parcel/goods trips.

## Test login and walk-through (THUMB RULE: test login per module)
No new app or role: `test.customer` and `test.truckdriver` (mini truck) already exist.
Walk-through (needs the mini truck priced, see below):
1. As test.customer, Home → the door says **Transport** with the truck picture.
2. Tap it → "Transport · by 1CallFix" header, four or fewer big buttons (only priced vehicles).
3. Tap **Shift house** → step 3 says "What are you moving?", House shifting picked, "+ 2 helpers" lit, price shown.
4. Tap **Send a parcel** → back to the normal parcel form (sizes, no helpers).
5. Book a mini truck (cash) → test.truckdriver gets the offer "Mini truck · 2 helpers".
