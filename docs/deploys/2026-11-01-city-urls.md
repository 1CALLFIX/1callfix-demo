## What's in it
Owner brief 2026-11-01 (city URL architecture). Every city gets the same addresses; no city name is in the code.

| Address | Page |
|---|---|
| `/` | homepage (unchanged), follows the city the visitor picked |
| `/{city}` | **new: city hub** — one door per business on in that city (Admin → Modules) |
| `/{city}/services` | the city's services list (this was `/{city}` until now) |
| `/{city}/services/{slug}` | a category or a service (this was `/{city}/{slug}`) |
| `/{city}/shop` | the shop (unchanged) |
| `/{city}/parcel` | **new: parcel page** for the city; "Send a parcel" goes to the existing `/parcel` |

- **Old links keep working.** `/{city}/{slug}` → 301 → `/{city}/services/{slug}`, query string kept (UTM, gclid).
  An old city slug and an old item slug still take one hop. `/categories` → the city's `/services` page (or the
  city chooser when more than one city is live). `/categories/{slug}` and `/services/{id|slug}` still 301 as before.
- **Reserved words**: `services`, `shop`, `parcel`, `food`, `grocery`, `pharmacy`, `taxi`, `hotels`, `rentals` can
  never be a city, category or service slug. `/{city}/food` etc. are a plain 404 until those businesses get a page.
- **Test shops are never public.** A shop whose seller signs in with an `@1callfix.test` email (the `test-accounts`
  Test Shop) is hidden from the shop pages, homepage, search engines and sitemap, and its items cannot be added to a
  cart by a normal customer. A signed-in test account still sees it, so the walk-through works on the live site.
  **Effect today:** the public `/nellore/shop` shows no products until a real seller is approved.
- **Search engines**: a page is indexed (and in the sitemap) only when it has real content — hub: any business real;
  services: the existing provider-coverage rule; shop: a real approved product; parcel: Parcel on and a base fare
  set. The city sitemap now also lists the real shops and their products. Canonical tags follow the new addresses.
- **Homepage shop rows** (only where the shop is on in the visitor's city, only real shops): *Shop by category*,
  *Shops near you*, *From local shops* (product cards with Add). Each row: on/off and order in Admin → Homepage
  sections; words in Website text & language (Homepage). New text group *City pages* for the hub and parcel page.
- Breadcrumbs: Home › City › Services › Category. Header menu, bottom menu and the Services door go straight to
  `/{city}/services`.
